2 training-selected SKUs · 28 training days (2020-01-01–2020-01-28) · 4 evaluation days (2020-01-29–2020-02-01) · £18,000 daily purchasing allowance · 4-day lead · 7-day review.

At least one policy meets the illustrative recorded-sales gate; this is not deployment approval.

Recorded sales are not unconstrained demand. This replay tests hypothetical fulfillment of recorded positive sales; it cannot estimate true historical lost sales or realized savings. This page uses invented fixture rows, not actual UCI data.

What is observed vs assumed?

  • Observed input fields: invoices, product codes, timestamps, gross quantities, selling prices, and country.
  • Training-only SKU selection and assumed acquisition cost: quantity-weighted training selling price × 0.5. No supplier purchase prices are observed.
  • Hypothetical inputs: starting stock, supplier lead time, purchasing allowance, holding charge, order fee, shortage penalty and replenishment policies.
  • Cancellations and negative quantities are exported separately, not silently netted into demand.
  • Absent product-day records are zero-filled. Closures, missing logs and historical stockouts cannot be distinguished.
  • Selection favors high-volume training products; this is not representative of the full catalog.
