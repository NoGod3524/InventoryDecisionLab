This diagnostic view does not change the frozen decision. Bars show mean unit fill. Amber line: 95% service target. Error bars: conditional 95% bootstrap interval for mean fill. Inspect per-seed service shares before making any operational recommendation.

Business interpretation

  • Forecast-aware replenishment may improve service under the base synthetic assumptions.
  • Budget restrictions can dominate forecasting differences. A failed service target should stop deployment, not be hidden.
  • Review frequency interacts with the daily non-carrying procurement allowance; changing it changes purchasing opportunities.
  • Lost-demand visibility, supplier variability, cost proxies, and real customer behavior require validation before a pilot.
