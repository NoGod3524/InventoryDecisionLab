Historical recorded-sales replay

24 training-selected SKUs · 56 training days (2010-12-01–2011-01-25) · 180 evaluation days (2011-01-26–2011-07-24) · £18,000 daily purchasing allowance · 4-day lead · 7-day review.

No policy reaches the 95% recorded-sales fulfillment gate under these assumptions.

Recorded sales are not unconstrained demand. This replay tests hypothetical fulfillment of recorded positive sales; it cannot estimate true historical lost sales or realized savings. Public source: UCI Online Retail (Chen, 2015), CC BY 4.0.

Gross positive sales retain positive orders even when separate cancellation records exist. A completely canceled large training order can distort product selection and initial inventory; inspect the matched-cancellation diagnostic before interpreting results.

One historical path is not multiplied into artificial independent seeds. No statistical interval is presented for this deterministic replay.

What is observed vs assumed?

  • Observed input fields: invoices, product codes, timestamps, gross quantities, selling prices, and country.
  • Training-only SKU selection and assumed acquisition cost: quantity-weighted training selling price × 0.5. No supplier purchase prices are observed.
  • Hypothetical inputs: starting stock, supplier lead time, purchasing allowance, holding charge, order fee, shortage penalty and replenishment policies.
  • Cancellations and negative quantities are exported separately, not silently netted into demand.
  • Absent product-day records are zero-filled. Closures, missing logs and historical stockouts cannot be distinguished.
  • Selection favors high-volume training products; this is not representative of the full catalog.
